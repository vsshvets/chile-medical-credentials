 ACCREDITATION OF UNDERGRADUATE MEDICAL EDUCATION 

 In accordance with the provisions of Law No. 20.129, the undergraduate programs leading to the professional titles of Medical Surgeon shall be mandatorily subjected to the accreditation process established by CNA-Chile. 
 The accreditation of undergraduate medical education shall entail the assessment and verification of compliance with quality criteria, with the aim of certifying the quality of the careers and programs offered by autonomous institutions of higher education, in accordance with the stated purposes of the institution delivering them and the current regulations governing their practice.
